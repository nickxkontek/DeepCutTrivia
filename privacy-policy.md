Deep Cut Trivia ("the app") is made by [your name]. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## The short version

- There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
- Games run on your own WiFi. Answers and player names stay on the host device and the players' devices.
- If you use fresh questions, the topics you choose are sent to our question service, which asks Claude (made by
  Anthropic) to write questions. Pack purchases are checked with Apple or Microsoft and added to a random balance code.

## Information the app uses

**Player names and answers.** Names typed when joining, and the answers chosen, travel only over your local WiFi
between the players' devices and the host device. They are kept only for the current session and are not sent to us.

**Game settings and saved questions.** Your settings, any questions you add yourself, and questions Claude has
written for you are stored on the host device so you can use them again. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Fresh questions and question packs.** When a host plays with fresh questions:

- The app sends our question service the chosen topics and themes, the difficulty levels, and the text of recent
  questions (so new ones don't repeat them).
- The service sends that request to Anthropic's Claude API to write the questions. Anthropic processes it under its
  commercial terms; see anthropic.com/legal for how Anthropic handles API data. The service doesn't keep the topics or questions.
- Your balance is stored on our service under a random **balance code** (like DCT-ABCD-EFGH-JKLM-NPQR-STUV). The code
  isn't linked to your name or email. On Apple devices it's kept in your iCloud so it follows you to your other
  devices; on Windows it's kept with the app. You can see and copy it in the app's shop.
- When you buy a pack, the app sends us the store's purchase details so we can confirm the purchase with Apple or
  Microsoft and add it to your balance once. We keep a record of each purchase ID, the product, and the balance code it
  was added to. We never receive your name, email, Apple ID, Microsoft account or payment details. The stores handle all payments.

**Local network.** The host device runs a small web server on your WiFi so other players can join. That's why the
app asks for Local Network permission (Apple) or allows incoming connections on its game ports (Windows). Nothing on your network is scanned or collected.

## Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Player names are typed by players, travel only on the local WiFi, and are not sent to us.

## Your choices

- You can play with the built-in questions without sending anything to our service.
- Question packs are one-time purchases; nothing renews.
- To have your balance code and its purchase records deleted, email us the code.
- To ask a question about this policy, contact [your email address].
